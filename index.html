What We Do

Practice Areas

Focused counsel across the full life of a HUD-insured multifamily construction project.

Construction Contract Review & Negotiation

Reviewing and negotiating HUD construction contracts — lump-sum and cost-plus — and the general conditions as modified by HUD's supplementary conditions, so risk is allocated fairly before you sign.

Davis-Bacon & Labor Standards

Prevailing-wage compliance, wage determinations, and certified payroll on federally assisted jobs — and defending against labor-standards and restitution exposure.

Cost Certification & Payment

Guiding contractors through HUD cost certification, requisitions and draws, retainage, and the change-order process — so you're paid for the work you've done.

Claims, Liens & Disputes

Change-order and delay claims, mechanic's-lien rights and waivers, and disputes that arise over the life of a HUD-insured project.
